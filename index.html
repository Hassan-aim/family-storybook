A Love Story That Began Within Family

My parents' story began through family relationships. My mother's parents approached my father's parents with the idea of marriage. At that time, my father rejected the proposal.

But the story did not end there. Later, my father himself came to my mother and asked her:

“Will you marry me?”

My mother immediately replied:
“Yes, I will marry you.”

That simple "yes" became the beginning of their life together. They got married when neither of them had a job. Their journey was not easy. They started their married life with dreams but without financial security.

Later, after I was born, both of them worked hard to build a better future. My mother became a nurse. My father became an electrician. My mother's education was also supported by my father. He paid her educational fees and encouraged her to continue studying. My mother struggled through her studies, worked hard, and eventually achieved her goal of getting a job.

Their story is not simply about marriage. It is about:

Love. Trust. Sacrifice. Support. Hard work. Growing together.

After about a year, my younger sister was born: N. Sai Pallavi. Later, my younger brother was born: N. Sai Manikanta. And their family became complete.

Their First Dream

N. SAI BHARGAVI

I am Sai Bhargavi, the first child of N. Vijayalakshmi and N. Usenappa.

From childhood, my parents worked hard to give us a disciplined and independent life.

For some periods of my childhood, I stayed with my Pini and Babai, while my parents were taking care of my younger sister. Those memories became an important part of my childhood.

I completed my schooling at TSWRS.

After completing my 10th class, my father suggested that I join MPC. At that time, I chose a different path. I joined Diploma in Computer Science Engineering at BDG College.

I completed my diploma successfully with 91%.

That achievement was not mine alone. Behind it were my parents' sacrifices, their encouragement, their discipline, their financial support, and their belief in me.

Today I am continuing my education in B.Tech — Information Technology at MECS.
